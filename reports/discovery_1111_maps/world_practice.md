## 1. Speed figures and ratings
- Beyer: final time, adjusted for distance par and a daily track variant - DRF, US - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyer_Speed_Figure
- Ragozin Sheets: time and pace, variant, ground loss (paths wide), weight, wind; lower is better; figure-pattern reading (bounce, tops: unverified) - https://www.americasbestracing.net/gambling/2020-horse-racing-speed-figures-explained
- Thoro-Graph: time, beaten lengths, ground lost/saved, weight, wind, hand-made variant; "Race Shapes" = each horse's adjusted first-quarter time, mean of last 3 - https://www.equibase.com/premium/eqbProductPage.cfm?pid=50304
- Brisnet E1/E2/Late Pace: speed to first call, second call, call-to-finish, 2 points a length; turn time by subtraction - https://support.brisnet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360056540451-BRIS-PACE-RATINGS-FAQ
- Equibase/TrackMaster power, speed, class and pace ratings - https://www.equibase.com/products/whatistrackmaster.cfm
- Racing Post Ratings: handicappers' weight-adjusted figures, re-adjusted overnight for trip/ground/yard form (forum account) - https://www.geegeez.co.uk/analysing-racing-post-ratings-uk-flat/ ; Topspeed (clock figure), Timeform pound-scale ratings and symbols, BHA official marks - methods unverified.

## 13. Syndicates and computer teams
- Benter 1994 (read directly): factors = current condition (recent runs, days off, workouts, age); past performance (position, lengths, normalised times); adjustments (strength of opposition, weight, jockey's contribution, bad luck, post position); today (weight, jockey, post); preferences (distance, surface, wet/dry, track). Distance preference = slope of past-race regression residuals on distance similarity, divided by its standard error. Multinomial logit; second-stage logit with public odds; Harville corrected by exponents .81/.65; half or third Kelly - https://www.gwern.net/doc/statistics/decision/1994-benter.pdf
- Woods: track, form, weather - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Woods_(gambler)
- Rebates on losing bets to high-volume players - https://www.scmp.com/article/992663/rebates-have-sting-tail
- US computer-assisted wagering: one club about 30% of wagering; late odds moves; win pool closed to them 2 minutes out - https://www.thoroughbreddailynews.com/computer-assisted-wagering-different-worlds-different-fixes/ ; https://www.truenicks.com/articles/286121/del-mar-to-close-win-pools-to-caw-two-minutes-to-post
