# The greyhound metrics registry

Every column of the point-in-time metrics tables (greyhound/pit/), generated by `python -m greyhound.pit --registry`. Each is known before the run's day; card-unsafe columns are known only shortly before the off (the weigh-in). Coverage and the scores on both targets are in each table's meta and the fast-loop reports.
