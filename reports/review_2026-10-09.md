# Full review, 9 Oct 2026: what happened to the model

The owner asked, on 9 Oct: "what has happened to our model, it was performing so well".

**The short answer.**
- The prediction model has not changed since 30 Sep, and it has not got worse.
- Its morning prices ranked the market and found value on 6-8 Oct as well as they did on 29 Sep-5 Oct.
- The losses since 7 Oct came from how we traded on those prices: the rule, not the model. Three changes:
  1. From 6 Oct 15:25 UK the trader read the closing model fitted with each runner's matched money, because the live
     key's feed reports it.
  2. The stake went to GBP400 to win, with no day limit from 7 Oct and no stop from 8 Oct.
  3. Most of the extra money went into top-ups, and the top-ups lost.
- The rule changed back on 9 Oct (the no-volume model, top-ups under the GBP4,000 day limit). It trades for the first
  time on 10 Oct.

Sources: TRADING.md (the day-by-day record) and reports/research_ledger.jsonl. Each figure below names its ledger tag.

## 4. What lost, and why

1. **The closing model with volume, on the live feed** (ledgers `live-review-1008`, `closing-correction-1008`).
   - The trader backs a horse when the closing model expects the BSP to come in at least 3% below the price taken.
   - The volume model was fitted on history where each runner's matched money was known. Read live, it trusted our
     price more than the market justified.
   - For the horses we backed, the BSP came out above the model's expectation by a mean 18% on the volume model
     (22% on 8 Oct), against 10% on the no-volume model.
   - Since 6 Oct 15:25 UK the volume model's backs ran at -3.9% CLV. The no-volume model made +3.6% up to that point.
2. **More money, later in the day.**
   - With no day limit the stakes doubled, from about GBP3,900 a day to GBP7,400-8,100.
   - The extra went on backs later in the day. By the UK hour a horse was entered, backs at 08:00-09:59 make +6-8%
     CLV, while backs from 12:00 lose (-6% at 13:00) (`time-cutoff-replay-1008`, `closing-model-by-hour-1007`).
3. **Top-ups.**
   - On 8 Oct the first backs were GBP1,122 at -5.4%, and top-ups were 86% of the stake.
   - On 9 Oct the first backs were GBP867.77 at **+6.9%**. The top-ups were GBP4,010.62 at **-2.2%**: the morning's
     first backs were good, and the volume model's top-ups lost even in the morning.
4. **What did not cause it.**
   - The predictions (section 2).
   - The BSP itself: it is calibrated across 2016-2026 (`bsp-calibration-1007`).
   - Betfair's commission: 2% throughout.
   - Withdrawals: they cut 4 Oct's stake, but not 7-9 Oct's.

## 6. The rule in force from 10 Oct, and its evidence

The rule:
- `closing_clv`, backing at +3% expected CLV, to win GBP400, at most GBP300 a bet.
- Every race is read by the closing model fitted **without** volume (`closing_volume: false`), so no runner is held
  to the GBP100 matched floor.
- Top-ups are on.
- At most GBP4,000 of stakes a day.
- Trading runs from 08:00 UK to 15 minutes before each off.

Its evidence:
- It is the rule of 30 Sep-5 Oct (+GBP513.98, CLV about +3.6%) at GBP400 to win instead of GBP250.
- It leads the replay over 1-8 Oct.
- It is backed by the five-month backtest of the no-volume model: +5.3% CLV, November 2025 to March 2026,
  `to-win-five-months-novol-1003`.

The trader's 06:50 UTC session on 10 Oct is its first live day. 9 Oct's restart placed nothing, because the day's
stakes were already past GBP4,000.

## 8. Risks

- **Not yet seen live.** The rule has traded no live day. Its evidence is the replay (which fills more kindly than
  the market did on 7-8 Oct) and 30 Sep-5 Oct at a smaller stake.
- **The day limit may be spent early, on top-ups.** On 9 Oct the old rule staked GBP4,878 by 10:19 UK, and 82% of it
  was top-ups at -2.2%. If the no-volume model tops up as fast, the GBP4,000 is gone by mid-morning and the day rests
  on the top-ups' CLV. Morning top-ups under the limit made +7% in the 7 Oct replay; 9 Oct's real ones did not.
- **The account.**
  - GBP4,888.75 against a replay that assumes GBP5,000.
  - GBP400 to win at long prices binds the funds first: 62 backs were refused for funds on 8 Oct.
- **The model's slope.** At 1.11 from 6 Oct (1.06 before), the model is a little more extreme than the market at the
  edges. To be watched with the 9-10 Oct price files.
